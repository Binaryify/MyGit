5c70df3 feat: everything you reach for

Everything you reach for, nothing you don't.

feat(staging)

Stage what you mean.

Stage files, changes or single lines. A changed file shows whole, so you can fix a typo right in the diff and save — then stage just the lines you want.

feat(merge)

Resolve conflicts side by side.

Ours, the result and theirs in three panes. Take a side or both, a conflict at a time, edit the result, and continue the merge, rebase or cherry-pick from the bar on top.

feat(branches)

Find any branch.

Search local and remote branches as you type. Switch, merge, rebase, compare, rename or delete from each one's menu — and interactively rebase from any commit.

feat(open)

Open anything.

Drop a folder on a window, clone, start a new repository, or run mygit . in a terminal. Each repository gets a window of its own.

  • logSearch the history by message, author or hash; filter by branch or path
  • diffSide-by-side diffs with syntax highlighting; compare two commits with ⌘-click
  • rebase -iReorder, reword, squash, fixup or drop, by dragging
  • stashStash with untracked files; apply, pop or branch from a stash
  • blameWho changed each line, and a double-click to their commit
  • pushPreview what a push sends; force with lease; sign-ins asked in the window
